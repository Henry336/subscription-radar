## Scope and mode

`index.html` is the single public product surface. Its first viewport has a
Persuade job—make the local-first mechanism and next action obvious—while the
analysis workspace has an Operate job. Operation wins whenever expression and
task clarity conflict.

## Audience, job, and action

Privacy-conscious individuals arrive with a bank-exported CSV or pasted
transactions. They need to understand what was detected, correct mistakes,
choose actions, and export a cleanup plan. The primary first action is “Use my
CSV”; “Run the private demo” is the no-risk proof path.

## Proof and constraints

Proof is the working transformation from transaction rows to explainable
recurring groups, renewal timing, and a selected-action queue. All demo values
must be labelled illustrative. Processing stays browser-only, with no account,
remote storage, analytics, external fonts, or third-party scripts. Estimates
must remain estimates.

## Chosen direction

The Signal Desk combines a near-black particle-detector field with warm
annotated ledger surfaces. A single scan sweep reveals recurring signals; the
ledger then connects each signal to evidence and a user decision. The system
uses sharp instrument geometry, tabular data, sparse acid-lime signal marks,
and coral urgency marks.

## Memorable moment

Statement rows visibly resolve into radar signals and then into a ranked action
queue. The radar is functional and accessible, not decoration.
