## Blockers resolved

- Suggestions no longer silently become user decisions; every active signal starts at **Needs review**.
- Export stays disabled until every active signal has an explicit action, with an explicit batch-accept path for power users.
- The import preview is a required consent checkpoint before findings appear.
- The initial demo no longer autoloads, restoring a clear before/after moment.
- Findings are compact, one-at-a-time disclosures with progress and mobile section jumps.
- The radar visibly explains timing, annual cost, heuristic score, and high-cost color encoding, and links every point/list item to its finding.
- Statement currency is explicitly label-only with no exchange-rate conversion.
- Clear requires confirmation; dismiss/restore recover focus and announce state.
- The scanner skip link, drop-zone focus, mobile privacy label/touch targets, and contrast miss were corrected.
- Probability-like “Confidence” language was replaced by **heuristic pattern score**.

## Remaining non-blocking deductions

- No dedicated keyboard accelerators beyond native controls and batch acceptance.
- Inline help is strong, but there is no searchable task-indexed help center.
- The deliberately information-rich authored instrument is denser than a perfectly minimal utility.

## Direction verdict

“The Signal Desk” is specific and coherent: a dark functional detector resolves into a warm paper evidence ledger and explicit decision queue. Privacy claims match the static architecture, estimates are qualified, and no bank-linking or cancellation capability is fabricated.
